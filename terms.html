The product

SoldScope is a Chrome extension that adds sorting and sales statistics to eBay search pages. The core features are free. SoldScope Pro is an optional paid license key that unlocks additional features inside the same extension.

Payments

Pro is sold through Lemon Squeezy, which acts as the merchant of record and handles payment, sales tax and VAT. Your license key is delivered by Lemon Squeezy by email right after purchase. Subscriptions renew automatically until you cancel, which you can do at any time from the link in your receipt email.

Refunds

If Pro does not work for you, email soldscope.help@gmail.com within 14 days of a purchase or renewal and we will refund it in full. No questions asked.

What we do not promise

SoldScope reads information that eBay shows in your browser. eBay can change its pages at any time, and numbers such as sell-through rate and median sold price are estimates from the listings eBay returns. They are not financial advice. If a change on eBay breaks a feature, we aim to fix it quickly; if we cannot, you can ask for a refund of the current period.

Acceptable use

Use SoldScope in line with eBay’s and Poshmark’s terms. SoldScope only loads pages you could open yourself, one extra page per search. Do not use it to scrape sites at scale.
